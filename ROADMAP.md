# Roadmap

## 0.0.3.x — DOCDB + official-source verification
- DOCDB worldwide master retrieval.
- USPTO ODP, KIPRIS Plus and INPI official second-pass adapters.
- EP primary-official verification through EPO OPS.
- Field-level provenance, conflict recording and source snapshots.
- Add WIPO PCT Webservice adapter when licensed credentials are available.
- Add additional national-office machine adapters only where documented and permitted.

## 0.0.4.x — patent family and legal intelligence
- DOCDB simple family + INPADOC extended family.
- Legal-event normalization and jurisdiction-specific status model.
- Applicant/assignee normalization and aliases.
- Citation network and jurisdiction coverage matrix.

## 0.0.5.x — structured technology routes
- Host, substrate, precursor, gene, enzyme, pathway, product and performance fields.
- Evidence-linked route extraction and technology ontology.

## 0.0.6.x — claims / FTO
- Independent-claim element extraction.
- Claim overlap matrix.
- FTO pre-screening by jurisdiction and legal status.

## 1.0.0.x — stable professional release
- Stable schema/migrations.
- Connector contracts and regression corpus.
- Reproducible project manifests and full provenance.
